# BFN app privacy policy

**Last updated:** 18 October 2026

> **The BFN app isn't available yet.** This policy describes how the app will handle your information from the start of its beta test. We'll update it as the app is built, and the date above will change each time.

## The short version

- We collect only what the app needs to work: your account, your profile and what you choose to share.
- You decide who sees what you share.
- We never sell your information, never show you adverts and never track you across other apps or websites.
- Your information is stored in London, UK.
- You can see, download, correct or delete your information at any time. Deleting your account deletes your profile, posts, photos and videos.
- You must be 18 or over to use the app.
- Questions? Email [bfnmovement@gmail.com](mailto:bfnmovement@gmail.com).

The rest of this page gives the detail.

## Who we are

The BFN app is made by **BFN (Built From Nothing, Built Together)**, a community built around connection, encouragement, mindset and movement.

BFN is responsible for your personal information in the app. In data protection law, that makes us the "data controller". You can contact us about anything on this page at [bfnmovement@gmail.com](mailto:bfnmovement@gmail.com). We're a small organisation, so we don't have a data protection officer: your questions come straight to us.

## What we collect and why

| What we collect | Why we need it | Our legal reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Your account:** your email address, your password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it, not even us) and a member number the app creates for you | To create your account, let you sign in and send you account emails, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password | **Contract:** we need it to provide the app you signed up for |
| **Your profile:** your name, profile photo, bio and privacy settings | To show your profile to other members, in the way your privacy settings allow | **Contract** |
| **What you share:** your posts, photos, videos and comments | To show them to the people you choose | **Contract** |
| **Your connections:** who you follow, your reactions to posts and who you've blocked | To build your feed, show your reactions, and keep members you've blocked away from you | **Contract** |
| **Reports:** what you report and why, reports other members make about your content, and what we did about them | To keep the community safe and act on harmful content | **Legitimate interests:** keeping members safe. Sometimes the law also requires us to act |
| **Security records:** your IP address (the internet address your phone uses), your type of phone and when you signed in | To keep accounts secure, spot misuse and fix problems | **Legitimate interests:** keeping the app and your account secure |
| **Emails you send us** | To answer you and act on requests about your information | **Legitimate interests**, or a **legal obligation** when you use your data rights |

"Legitimate interests" means we have a good reason that you'd reasonably expect, and we've checked that it doesn't override your privacy.

**What you have to give us:** an email address and a password to create an account, and a name for your profile. Without them we can't create your account. Everything else, like a photo, a bio or posts, is up to you.

**Information from other members:** most of what we hold comes from you. Some comes from other members, for example when they react to or comment on your posts, or report something you've shared.

**What we don't collect:** your location, your phone's contacts, health data from your phone or watch, or advertising identifiers. The app doesn't use advertising or analytics tools. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy first.

### Sensitive things you choose to share

BFN is a place where you can talk about difficult days as well as good ones, so you might choose to share something about your mental or physical health. That's always your choice. We only use what you share to show it to the people you've chosen, and we don't analyse your posts to work out anything about you. Before sharing something personal, check who can see the post. Your privacy settings let you choose.

## Who else handles your information

We use a small number of services to run the app. They only use your information to provide their service to us.

- **Supabase** stores member accounts, the app's database, and photos and videos, on Amazon Web Services computers in London. It also sends account emails. Supabase is a US company. Its contract with us includes the safeguards UK law requires for any access to your information from outside the UK.
- **Expo** builds the app and delivers app updates to your phone. When the app checks for an update, Expo receives your IP address, your phone's operating system and a random code that isn't linked to your BFN account. Expo is based in the United States.
- **Apple and Google** run the App Store and Google Play. When you download the app, they handle that, and your store account, under their own privacy policies.
- **Google (Gmail)** handles the emails you send to our email address.

We'll also share information if the law requires it, for example a court order, or when we believe it's necessary to protect someone's life or safety.

**We never sell your information.**

## Where your information is stored, and how it's protected

Your information is stored in **London, UK**. It's encrypted while it travels between your phone and our database, and while it's stored.

Encryption protects your information from outsiders. It doesn't hide it from BFN. We can see what's stored when we need to, for example to look into a report.

Inside the app, rules in the database decide what each member can see and change. For example, only you can edit your own posts, and members you've blocked can't see you.

## How long we keep it

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Your account and profile | Until you delete your account |
| Your posts, photos, videos, comments and reactions | Until you delete them, or delete your account |
| Blocks | Until you unblock the member, or either of you deletes your account |
| Reports | Up to 1 year after we close the report, so we can deal with repeated abuse. Longer only if we need them for a legal claim or to protect someone's safety |
| Security records | Up to 90 days |
| Emails you send us | As long as we need to deal with your message, and no more than 2 years |
| Backup copies | Information you delete is removed from our backups within 30 days |

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from the app's settings, or by emailing us. When you do:

- your account and profile disappear from the app straight away
- your posts, photos, videos, comments, reactions, follows and blocks are deleted
- everything is erased from our systems within 30 days, apart from reports and security records, which we keep for the times shown above

The full steps are on our [delete your account page]({{ '/delete-account/' | relative_url }}).

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

- **get a copy** of the information we hold about you
- **correct** information that's wrong
- **delete** your information
- **download** what you've shared in a format you can use elsewhere
- **object** to us using your information for our legitimate interests
- **ask us to limit** how we use your information

To use any of these rights, email [bfnmovement@gmail.com](mailto:bfnmovement@gmail.com). It's free. We'll reply within one month, and we may need to check it's really you first.

We don't make decisions about you by computer alone that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us, so we can try to put it right. You can also complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**: [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or 0303 123 1113.

## Age

You must be **18 or over** to use the BFN app. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we'll delete it.

## Cookies and this website

The app doesn't use cookies or advertising trackers.

This website doesn't use cookies or analytics either. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security ([GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).

When we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
