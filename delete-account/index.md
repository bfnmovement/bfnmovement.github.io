# Delete your BFN account

This page explains how to delete your account in the **BFN** app, made by **BFN (Built From Nothing, Built Together)**, and what happens to your information.

> **The BFN app isn't available yet.** These steps apply from the start of its beta test.

## In the app

Go to the app's settings and choose **Delete account**. You'll be asked to confirm.

## By email

If you can't use the app, email [bfnmovement@gmail.com](mailto:bfnmovement@gmail.com) **from the email address you use for your BFN account**, with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We'll reply to confirm, and delete your account within 30 days.

## What's deleted

- your account and profile (name, photo, bio and settings)
- your posts, photos, videos and comments
- your reactions, follows and blocks

Your account and profile disappear from the app straight away. Everything is erased from our systems within 30 days.

## What we keep, and for how long

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Reports you made, or reports about your content, and what we did about them | Up to 1 year after we close the report, so we can deal with repeated abuse |
| Security records (IP address, type of phone, sign-in times) | Up to 90 days |
| Emails you sent us | No more than 2 years |
| Backup copies | Removed within 30 days |

## Deleting some of your information without deleting your account

You can delete any of your posts, photos, videos or comments in the app at any time. To ask us to delete something else, email [bfnmovement@gmail.com](mailto:bfnmovement@gmail.com).

More detail is in our [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
